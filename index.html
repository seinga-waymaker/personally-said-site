FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get the recording?

1–3 business days from when the last message comes in. We'll always tell you the exact date when you order.

How long do people have to leave a message?

As long as they need — most messages run 30 seconds to 2 minutes. There's no strict cutoff, but shorter is usually sweeter.

What if the line's busy when someone calls?

It won't be — the line can take calls at the same time from different people. If someone ever has trouble getting through, they can text the same number instead and it'll still make it into the recording.

What format do I get the recording in?

An MP3 file you can play on your phone, computer, or send to anyone — no app needed to open it, same as any other audio file.

Do I need an app to call or text in a message?

No. Just a phone. Call or text the number — that's it.

Is there a limit to how many people can leave a message?

No limit. Invite everyone who has something to say.

What if someone leaves something inappropriate or off-topic?

We listen to every message before it goes into the final recording, so anything that doesn't belong gets left out.

Can I hear the recording before it's sent to the recipient?

Yes — let us know if you'd like a preview before it goes out.
